**Project:** Latent Causal Infeasibility for Long-Horizon TAMP

**Deliverable:** One unified 2D/3D method that generates a **single initial-scene set of candidate object relocations**, constructs complete **ordered** corrective sequences, scores them with the existing coupled `F2/F3/F5` Boltzmann model, and selects **one** predicted low-cost, physically successful repair—or abstains.

## 0. Read this first: decisions and non-goals

### 0.1 Decisions now fixed for the core study

1. **Exactly two primary learned responsibilities:** a conditional placement **cVAE** proposes object–destination actions; the **coupled Boltzmann model** scores full ordered repairs. The existing separate travel readout, if used, remains an auxiliary cost predictor rather than a new causal/repair model.
2. **Change the question learned by the cVAE.** The existing sampler learns positive **isolated placement** distributions from Q2; the new principal hypothesis trains a goal/task-conditioned sampler to generate placements that **participate in physically successful complete repairs**. Compare from-scratch, Q2-only, and Q2-initialized/combined training experimentally, without preselecting the winner.
3. **Train sequentially/coordinately, not with joint gradient flow.** Use GT X/Y evidence to supervise complete repair outcomes, not unverified Boltzmann scores as replacement truth. A sampler revision is followed by X/Y training-data alignment and Boltzmann retraining, not by a model that learns to exploit its own predictions.
4. **Only one proposal pass per original root.** Query the cVAE for all eligible objects **from the original scene** (in batches), construct candidate complete repairs of length 1–3, and score those sequences in batches. The optional empty repair is handled separately. Do **not** re-query either neural model at physical execution handoffs.
5. **No horizontal/vertical beam or prefix-priority search as the new method.** Candidate *construction* may be batched, enumerated, or budgeted; it is not a neural expansion policy or iterative search over hypothetical states. The Boltzmann model evaluates the whole ordered sequence, so physical handoff feasibility is learned implicitly from ordered X labels.
6. **Select exactly one best admissible complete repair.** Execute/verify that prediction independently for the primary metric. If its predicted order proves physically infeasible, record **predictor/pipeline failure** under the pinned teacher contract; do not conceal it with execution-time recovery or try the second-ranked repair in the same modified scene.
7. **The cVAE does not directly choose blockers or the final action order.** Candidate generation ranges over all eligible movable objects. The cost-aware Boltzmann choice determines which objects and ordered moves are actually selected. No learned blocker classifier, separate third repair network, joint-state handoff head, online IK/grasp oracle, or automatic return-home step.
8. **Retain the present finite action grammar**: up to three corrective relocations; each non-target object at most once; static support/legality checks; fixed objects stay; no initially occupied destination reuse/slot swaps; no active-target relocation; the original target task is executed last and is not one of the three corrective moves. Nonlinear freeing/reuse of newly available destinations is a **separate future extension**.
9. **No proof of global feasibility/optimality from the learned model.** Energy minimization is over the generated, scored candidate set only. Budget exhaustion/abstention and the legacy `NONE` category are not automatically certificates of universal physical impossibility.
10. **Maintain 2D/3D parity in semantics**, while using appropriate 8×8 versus 6×6 features, geometry, physical teachers, and separately fitted model weights.

### 0.2 What is explicitly postponed

- Iterative hypothetical resampling after object-pose edits, depth-balanced/beam/frontier search, and nonlinear unblocking/slot reuse.
- Physical execution-time replanning, fallback to second-ranked plan after partial execution, and intermediate handoff-state labels or q-state predictors.
- DPP subset selection, GAI/bandit arm identification, and CEM-style iterative sampler fitting as primary ablations. These were **considered**, not adopted. Revisit only with a documented empirical gap and a distinct testable hypothesis.
- Direct end-to-end gradients from Boltzmann through a discrete candidate generator; ungrounded score-only self-training; a separately learned blocker detector.
- Exhaustive labeling of all destinations, permutations, roots, or a universal infeasibility certificate.
- Claims of superiority over analytical samplers, uncoupled/direct predictors, or published TAMP baselines prior to actual comparisons.

### 0.3 Scientific question, hypothesis, and desired outcome

> **Can a task-aware cVAE learn to propose a small set of individually specified object relocations whose legal complete combinations allow an order-sensitive, coupled X/Y Boltzmann model to select a verified, low-cost repair with fewer proposal/scorer evaluations than existing Q2-only or simple broad sampling?**

Success is judged **end-to-end**, not by cVAE likelihood or Boltzmann NLL alone. Establish both **proposal-set coverage** and **conditional ranking/selection quality** under controlled budgets.

### 1.3 Exact label semantics; do not change

For immutable original root `R=(S0,q0,qref,T,g,...)` and ordered repair `r=[(o1,d1),...,(om,dm)]`, `0<=m<=3`:

- `X(r)=1` means the ordered corrective relocations are executable from the **original** root, carrying the achieved robot state through every action, **including the finalized free-gripper terminal connector to `qref`**. No home reset or intermediate learned robot-state labels.
- `Y(s(r))=1` means the original target task is independently feasible in the **final edited endpoint scene** from `qref` under the pinned Y teacher. `Y` depends on endpoint assignment/goal, not action permutation.
- `Z(r)=X(r) AND Y(s(r))` is the successful-repair outcome. Every `X=1,Y=1` pair is an evidence-backed successful complete correction for that candidate.
- `K0` is the no-repair case; `K1`–`K3` describe relative legacy minimal-verified correction classes. Do not feed `K_legacy` to either network.
- `NONE` means **no verified repair in the declared old search/teacher scope**. It is not a proof that no alternative object/destination/order can succeed. Keep abstention and true impossibility distinct.
- Missing/unqueried/timeout/administrative failures are neither positive evidence nor assumed physical impossibility. Track actual teacher budget, attempted status, witness/replay and negative type; distinguish a protocol-relative observed 0 from a sound universal certificate.

**Source-sensitive caveat:** Historical generators found a first verified primary repair and exited those primary loops, but did collect some additional strategic/negative/order tests afterward. Do not say each root contains literally only one query. Multiple successful permutations often reuse the **same** destination assignment; one assignment is one placement-learning example set, not six independent placement solutions.

## 2. Formal problem and exactly what is optimized

### 2.1 Candidate actions, set and complete repairs

Let `O_m` be *all eligible movable non-target objects*, determined from learner-visible fixed/mobile attributes and the declared action grammar (never GT blocker identities). A candidate action is `a=(o,d)` where `d` is a statically legal supported destination in the original scene.

For every object `o`, the sampler proposes a set `D_o` of **distinct** legal destinations, usually of size `M_o`:

\[
D=\bigcup_{o\in O_m}\{(o,d):d\in D_o\}.
\]

`R(D)` contains all statically valid ordered sequences of **distinct objects** of length 1, 2 or 3 using actions from `D`. Include an empty sequence separately for K0. Different object orders are distinct X candidates; identical endpoint assignments share Y. Do not put ground-truth blockers or legacy K into the construction logic.

With `n` eligible objects and equal `M` destinations each, an **unfiltered upper bound** is:

\[
|\mathcal R(D)| \le nM + n(n-1)M^2+n(n-1)(n-2)M^3.
\]

For `n=3,M=4`: `12 + 96 + 384 = 492` nonempty candidates before legality/dedup. Larger scenes demand a **declared scorer-evaluation cap**, but not a prefix-expansion search. Static mutual destination conflicts and forbidden initial occupancy must be filtered; do not allow newly vacated slot reuse in V3 core.

### 2.2 Coupled Boltzmann X/Y model (UNCHANGED)

For each unique endpoint `s=s(r)` the F3 branch emits `b_s`; for each **ordered** sequence `r`, F2 emits `a_r` and the retained F5 coupling emits `w_r`. At fixed temperature 1:

\[
P_\theta(Y_s=1)=\sigma(b_s),\qquad
P_\theta(X_r=1\mid Y_s=y)=\sigma(a_r+w_ry).
\]

Hence:

\[
\boxed{p_{11,\theta}(r)=P(X_r=1,Y_s=1)=\sigma(b_s)\,\sigma(a_r+w_r).}
\]

`P(X_r=1)` **marginally** is `pY * sigmoid(a+w) + (1-pY) * sigmoid(a)`; it is not simply `sigmoid(a)`. Keep the original exact root-consistent observed-data NLL, root/endpoint sharing, and missing-label marginalization. Do **not** replace the coupled model with a direct single-Z classifier or delete F5 merely because an ablation is null.

**Physical meaning of order:** the F2/F5 model learns whether the **entire ordered sequence** can execute and reach its contracted terminal `qref` connector. It does **not** learn explicit intermediate robot configurations, and it does not need Q2 labels of all physical handoffs. An incorrect predicted executable order counts against the model under identical teacher assumptions.

### 2.3 Final decision: one plan, zero retries

For each constructed complete candidate, optionally predict witnessed travel `Jhat(r)` and compute the analytic displacement `D(r)`. With nonnegative validation-tuned weights:

\[
C_\theta(r)= -\log\max(p_{11,\theta}(r),\varepsilon)
       +\alpha |r|+\beta D(r)+\gamma\widehat J(r).
\]

Keep the existing **no-op gate** for K0 and the calibrated feasibility admission threshold `eta`. When no correction is admissible, return **ABSTAIN / NO VERIFIED REPAIR PREDICTED**, not a certificate of universal infeasibility. Choose:

\[
r^* = \arg\min_{r\in\mathcal R(D),\;p_{11}(r)\ge\eta} C_\theta(r),
\]

subject to the preexisting no-op logic, deterministic tie breaks, and an explicit scorer budget. The selected repair is physically verified **once** from the untouched original root. There is no online model call after moving the first obstacle. Top-H alternatives may be retained for **offline analysis on independently reset roots**; they are not a physical backtracking policy.

**Meaning of 'optimal':** minimal predicted selection cost **within the generated/scored pool**, not global motion-optimal or proof of repairability.

### 2.4 What the candidate-set optimizer should achieve

Two independent necessary conditions:

\[
\operatorname{OracleCover}(D)=\max_{r\in\mathcal R(D)} Z^*(r)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\operatorname{Top1Success}(D,\theta)=Z^*\!\left(r^*_{\theta}(D)\right).
\]

They are physically labelled evaluation quantities **only where the relevant candidates have been checked**. A pool containing an unknown candidate is not proven unsuccessful. A measured, labelled subset provides a **lower bound on success-containing coverage**; avoid pretending every unqueried candidate failed.

The principal optimization is:

\[
\max_{\phi,\;\text{set-rule }\psi,\;\theta}
\mathbb E_{\text{roots}}\big[Z^*(r^*_{\theta}(D_{\phi,\psi}(S_0,T)))\big]
\quad\text{subject to fixed raw-proposal, unique-action, scored-sequence, and runtime budgets}.
\]

Use **sequential, blocked optimization** to approximate this objective: proposal supervision from *verified successful repairs*, selection-rule tuning on development outcomes, then physically labelled matching-distribution X/Y training. Tie-break task-success-equal methods by smaller compute/verified travel; do not optimize away success with an arbitrary cost weight.

**Failure attribution:** (A) useful repair missing from produced candidate/scored set = proposal/construction failure; (B) verified successful candidate present but another is selected or none is admitted = Boltzmann ranking/calibration/selector failure; (C) selected succeeds = success. Report any unverifiable portions as **unknown**, not category A.

## 3. Main cVAE hypothesis: learn useful complete-repair ingredients

### 3.1 Training target versus inference condition

**Old:** `p_phi(d | S, o)` trained on physically successful **Q2 isolated placements**. This is a proposal-density model, **not** a calibrated `P(physical_feasibility)` or a measure of downstream goal benefit.

**New:** `p_phi(d | S0, o, T, g)` (also include declared robot/shape/capability context available at inference). Train it to place mass on destinations that **occur in at least one observed X=1, Y=1 complete correction** for this root and target. At inference, the input is only observed `S0`, object `o`, `T,g`, known robot context and legality mask. Do **not** pass the GT action set, physically measured X/Y, teacher-selected blocker IDs, costs, outcomes, `K_legacy`, or future object states as inputs.

Interpretation: this is a **task-relevance-weighted placement proposal distribution**, not the probability that performing that move alone will solve the goal, nor the probability that the object is certainly a blocker. The Boltzmann model still judges whether an entire ordered sequence is executable and goal restoring.

### 3.2 Unique assignment is the unit of sampler supervision

Canonical **assignment key** for one root: sorted unique tuples `(object_id, destination_cell)`; it discards **order**, but not object identities, target goal or root. Exact ordered keys remain for X.

Example: the same `{A→12,B→31,C→45}` has four X-successes among six tested orders. For the cVAE, it is **one** positive placement-assignment example with three action ingredients; for Boltzmann, all six X labels are informative, sharing a single endpoint Y=1. Six permutations must **not** give that assignment six times the sampler weight.

A root may have one or many known successful assignments. **Primary minimalist variant:** choose **one verified X=1,Y=1 unique assignment per training root**, deterministically, with the selection rule fixed from TRAIN (e.g., stable hash of assignment among verified positives; alternatively separately ablate verified cheapest assignment when comparable witnessed cost is available). Do not use a special hidden intended blocker annotation.

**Secondary variant:** retain **all existing distinct verified successful assignments**, with **root-balanced and assignment-balanced weights**; do not assume more alternatives are automatically better. The teammate's new distinct assignments are an optional subsequent data expansion, **not a blocker for V3**.

If the root has no verified successful assignment, it contributes no **positive cVAE repair-destination** examples; it still provides physical X/Y negatives or missing observations for the Boltzmann model and abstention assessment. Do not treat every object omitted from a successful assignment, or every unobserved destination, as a negative placement label.

**Static-grammar consistency:** A physically successful recorded repair may use a destination that the **new V3 initial-scene grammar forbids** (for example, a cell originally occupied, even though vacated later). Do **not** quietly train the V3 task-conditioned cVAE to reconstruct that destination under a mask declaring it illegal. Audit each positive assignment against the V3 legal mask; retain excluded records in a versioned `outside_v3_grammar` ledger for the nonlinear extension and separately report the fraction of repair-supervision roots still usable. Do not silently change the legacy teacher labels or grammar to make those samples fit.

### 3.3 Correct conditional VAE training loss

For context `c=(S0,o,T,g,robot capability,legal mask)` and verified target destination `d`:

- Prior `p(z|c)` may remain standard normal `N(0,I)` initially (a conditional prior is optional, not mandatory).
- Recognition/posterior network `q_omega(z|c,d)` sees **d during training only**.
- Decoder `p_phi(d|z,c)` is a masked categorical distribution over legal cells. The same object encoder is permutation-invariant over scene objects; the queried-object identity feature and target/goal descriptor must be present and dimension-checked.

Per observation the **negative conditional ELBO** is:

\[
\ell_{\rm cVAE}(c,d)=\mathbb E_{q_\omega(z\mid c,d)}[-\log p_\phi(d\mid z,c)]
+\beta\,\mathrm{KL}(q_\omega(z\mid c,d)\,\|\,p(z\mid c)).
\]

Use reparameterization; mask only **statically illegal** destinations; do not mask physically hard or allegedly irrelevant destinations using GT. `beta` warm-up may follow the existing implementation, then be tuned only on validation.

With root `i` having observed successful assignment set `A_i^+`, the main task-aware training objective should avoid oversampling heavily labelled roots, long K3 assignments and six-success-order duplicates:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{\rm repair-cVAE}=\frac{1}{N_+}\sum_{i}\frac{1}{|A_i^+|}
\sum_{A\in A_i^+}\frac{1}{|A|}\sum_{(o,d)\in A}
\ell_{\rm cVAE}(S_i,o,T_i,g_i,d).}
\]

For the **one-assignment** variant `|A_i^+|=1`. If K-stratified root sampling is used, report both balanced and natural-frequency evaluation. Retain counts/provenance per assignment, but do not interpret six successful orders as six independent placement positives.

**Training comparisons required:**

- **Q2-only old goal-blind cVAE** (reproduce current baseline).
- **Repair-only, from-scratch task-conditioned cVAE** (user's key proposed method).
- **Q2-pretrained → repair-fine-tuned task-conditioned cVAE** (new goal input added at fine-tuning; initialize/regularize cleanly, not with fake repair labels).
- **Q2 + repair mixed/auxiliary training**, only if comparing a consistent goal-masked Q2 branch or compatible pretraining objective; Q2 records do not automatically provide task-relevance labels. Log the task-conditioning protocol in full.
- **One assignment per root vs all currently known unique assignments**, keeping weights/root splits and training budget comparable.

**Optional, not first pass:** weight each distinct verified successful assignment by legitimate *observed* cost (e.g., displacement, move count, witnessed travel), normalized **within root**. Use equal weights first. A single first-found teacher repair is not globally optimal; outcome-weighted objectives can encourage collapse onto one teacher style. No Boltzmann-score-only elite self-labeling.

### 3.4 Limit of independent individual-object proposals

A successful assignment can exhibit **complementarity**: A→12 is useful mainly when B→31 is also selected. The simple per-object cVAE learns *marginal* ingredients and may not preserve correlations. This is not a reason to introduce another network before running the experiment.

**Required diagnostic:** given a scene with at least one known positive assignment, ask whether the generated per-object sets jointly contain **every action ingredient of at least one verified successful assignment**; call this `KnownAssignmentIngredientRecall`. Then measure **physical whole-sequence oracle coverage** on a deliberately labelled candidate subset, because ingredient recall does not guarantee a correct order or X/Y success.

If marginals improve but compatible full sets remain absent, consider **one shared scene-level latent** or simple assignment-level sampling **within the same cVAE family** as a separately preregistered extension, not an assumed first-pass improvement. Do not condition the cVAE on a selected action order or a GT assignment at inference.

## 4. Candidate-set construction and sampling experiments

### 4.1 Direct construction algorithm (no hypothetical search)

```text
INPUT: initial root R=(S0,q0,qref,T,g,...), frozen sampler, frozen Boltzmann model,
       sampler/sequence budgets and cost/selection rule

1. Compute declared static legal initial-scene destinations for each eligible movable object.
2. Query the cVAE for each object's placements ONCE from S0, conditioned on object and goal.
   Produce distinct legal sets D_o; record raw draws, duplicates, pool sizes, and RNG seeds.
3. Construct all legal complete ordered sequences of length 1..3 from these sets:
   - unique objects per sequence; no target relocation or fixed-object motion;
   - no repeated/stay action; no initially occupied/goal/overlap destination;
   - distinct destinations for simultaneously moved objects;
   - preserve all order permutations as separate X candidates;
   - no true K, blocker list, physical calls, or unknown-feasibility oracle filtering.
4. Include empty sequence/no-op as a separate task feasibility candidate.
5. If candidate count exceeds the frozen B_score, use a declared, label-blind,
   reproducible BUDGETED CONSTRUCTION policy (see below); NEVER silently bias
   the comparison by letting one sampler score many more candidates.
6. Batch-score complete ordered candidates from ORIGINAL R, reusing endpoint Y
   scores where appropriate; compute p11, move count, displacement, optional Jhat.
7. Apply frozen no-op gate, eta admission threshold, and total cost C(r).
8. Return ONE lowest-cost admissible ordered repair or ABSTAIN.
9. For evaluation ONLY: independently query physical X and Y for the selected
   original-root repair; record truthful outcome, no execution-time retry.
```

**Important:** The X network may internally construct ideal object-pose **prefix representations** to encode order; that **does not** imply querying the cVAE again, running search, or inventing intermediate achieved `q`. Keep these distinct in code comments and paper diagrams.

### 4.2 What if full set construction is too large?

Use a **fixed construction/scorer budget**, not an energy-driven frontier. At the first implementation pass:

- Enumerate all valid sequences if the filtered count is under `B_score`.
- Otherwise sample/allocate **complete ordered candidates** with a deterministic seeded, label-blind rule balancing lengths `K1/K2/K3`, object combinations, destination-rank/region strata and order permutations, and deduplicate exact ordered keys. Include no-op independently. Avoid preferentially keeping only high cVAE-likelihood products, or only K1, because this can erase the very low-probability useful choices under study.
- Report the **allocation and cap exactly** (how many K1, K2 and K3 were constructed, actually scored, and physically labelled), with sensitivity analysis at multiple caps. Do not use GT K or X/Y labels to decide candidate admission at inference.
- If every chosen set fits within a modest cap, prefer **complete enumeration** for the first clean experiment: it removes candidate-pruning confounds.
- A cap that excludes combinations must be treated as part of the **candidate-construction method**; failures may arise from sampling, compatibility, or this budgeted composer. Log attribution separately if possible.

Suggested *pilot grids* (not fixed conclusions): per-object `M in {2,4,8,12,16}`; build `B_score` after measuring actual candidate-count distributions and GPU time at `M=4/8` for each domain. Hold the same `B_score`/latency target across methods for primary fair comparisons, and provide a curve as budget increases.

### 4.3 The sampling strategies we actually agreed to test

**S0 — current sampler, exact reproduction:** masked cVAE latent draws, repeated stochastic cell samples, first `M` unique legal cells (`raw_mult=8` in existing code), originally goal-blind, `M=4`. Use this to reproduce old data. **This is not literally probability top-k.**

**S1 — true top-M estimated marginal probability:** optionally estimate `p_phi(d|context)=E_z[p_phi(d|z,context)]` by a declared Monte Carlo sample budget, take M highest cells. Necessary if calling an arm “top-k”. This has extra decoder cost; account for it.

**S2 — without-replacement stochastic proposals:** sample unique cells from an estimated marginal categorical distribution (e.g., Gumbel-Top-K on the MC estimated marginal); compare directly with existing repeat-and-dedup under equal compute. Gumbel-Top-K applied independently to a single latent draw is not automatically top-k from the **marginal** cVAE distribution; specify which distribution is being sampled.

**S3 — temperature-controlled sampling:** `q_tau(d|c) ∝ p_phi(d|c)^(1/tau)`, on the same masked/estimated marginal; vary a small validation-chosen range near/above 1. Investigate whether retaining lower-density, goal-useful cells improves whole-repair coverage. Do not describe higher density as guaranteed greater physical placeability.

**S4 — cVAE + broad-coverage mixture:** `q_epsilon=(1-epsilon)p_phi + epsilon q_broad`, where `q_broad` is predeclared **legal-cell uniform or the existing distance-band stratified control**; respect exact static legality and mixture budget. Keep both a uniform and band-stratified reference if inexpensive (the band baseline was strong in old Q2 hit@4). No privileged swept-volume/teacher screening.

**S5 — task-conditioned cVAE:** same one-shot direct constructor and matched `M/B_score`, but the new repair-trained model (`Q2`, `repair-only`, `combined` as separate training arms), initially using the same simple stochastic-without-replacement readout as controls to isolate **distribution learning** from **sampling rule**.

**S6 — optional adaptive `M_o` (ablation only after S0–S5):** learn no new importance/blocker network. Allocate a fixed **total unique-action** budget `sum_o M_o <= B_actions` while satisfying `|R(D)| <= B_score`, `M_o` in bounded range. Candidate signals that can be tested without GT include goal-conditioned placement entropy, distinct-proposal count, accessible spatial diversity, and observable task geometry. **High entropy does not imply causal importance**; if adaptive allocation fails to improve end-to-end success at equal budget, drop it. Compare with uniform allocation and a simple deterministic round-robin cap.

**Explicit exclusions from primary study:** DPP, GAI, CEM, exhaustive online repeated resampling, and using Boltzmann predictions alone to declare cVAE-generated destinations positive. Analytical set-rule learning is optional *after* S0–S5; do not create a third neural ranker by stealth.

### 4.4 Top-K-next-batch and top-H are diagnostics, not deployment behavior

- “Try K actions, then take the next K if execution fails” is **not** the deployed framework: after partial execution the state is different and models are not conditioned/trained for new handoff roots.
- You may compare disjoint cVAE rank bands **offline from an identically reset original scene** to assess whether low-density placements add missing successful repairs.
- You may save sorted top-H complete repairs for an *oracle best-of-H* analysis on independent resets, but **deployed success is top-1 only**. Never equate oracle best-of-H with task success.

## 5. Coordinated SEQUENTIAL training (no gradient-through-Boltzmann)

### 5.1 Why training the two models independently is insufficient

A Q2 cVAE can be excellent at isolated placements but omit a goal-critical destination. A perfectly fitted Boltzmann model cannot rank an absent sequence. Conversely, a new task-aware cVAE can supply valid repairs that a Boltzmann network trained on the **old** proposal/search distribution misranks due to covariate shift. Simply increasing M might improve coverage but worsen top-1 due to extra false positives.

**Therefore separate four effects experimentally:** (i) sampler's learned distribution; (ii) sampling/subset rule; (iii) complete-sequence constructor and scorer budget; (iv) Boltzmann's matched-distribution training and calibrated cost selector.

### 5.2 Stage T0: read-only audit; TRAIN roots only for learning

Produce a canonical joined manifest across base X/Y, V2 additions and Q2 provenance. Per root, count:

- distinct `X=1,Y=1` **assignments** (unordered object/destination set) versus total successful **orders**;
- distinct positive destinations per object and root, distribution by K0/K1/K2/K3/NONE and object count;
- number of unique endpoints with Y known, X known, X/Y missing, teacher `negative_kind`, replay/witness status and cost availability;
- disagreement/incompatibility between legacy K, finalized X/Y and any V2 labels;
- known successful assignment **ingredient** coverage under current `M=4` proposal distribution using training-only repeated samples;
- exact root/family splits and teacher versions, prior on-policy candidate-generation provenance;
- initial goal/context feature availability in Q2 vs root X/Y. Never use teacher label fields as cVAE input.

**Keep the existing immutable base and V2 snapshots untouched.** Build a new additive V3 manifest with source hashes, root IDs and separate train/validation/test entries; validate family/parent lineage split disjointness and dedup over sources. `NONE` may remain protocol-relative.

### 5.3 Stage T1: baseline direct constructor with FROZEN V2 models

Implement the static one-shot direct constructor first. With **identical frozen V2 weights**, compare S0/S1/S2 and `M=4/8/12/16` (and S3/S4 if cheap) under matched candidate budgets. Produce:

- actual sampler raw draws/duplicates/unique actions, legal masks, per-root `n`, `M`, `|R(D)|`, scored sequences by length, model runtime;
- candidate-set ingredient recall against **known positive assignments** (may underestimate true coverage);
- physically verified top-1 success on a DEVELOPMENT subset (independent selected repair oracle verification); and
- a small controlled **fully labelled candidate audit** on chosen TRAIN/validation roots for actual proposal-versus-ranking attribution. Never assume unknown candidates are physically negative.

This phase answers whether sampling/constructing candidates, *without retraining anything*, is the bottleneck. It also validates that direct construction is not implicitly the previous `oneshot` tree.

### 5.4 Stage T2: train the TASK-CONDITIONED cVAE on AVAILABLE data

Build the new repair-assignment training view from `X=1,Y=1` TRAIN records. Keep `assignment_id` stable and `order_id` separate. Train **from scratch** and, for comparison, Q2-pretrained/fine-tuned models as defined in §3. Use one-assignment/root and existing all-distinct-assignment variants. Keep every K1/K2/K3 root eligible; exclude K0/NONE from positive repair-destination loss unless they contain separately verified new repairs. Their other observations remain valid for the X/Y learner.

Model selection uses **validation goal-aware proposal metrics**, chiefly known-assignment ingredient recall at fixed M and total scored-pool budget, plus physically labelled whole-repair coverage where available. Also report cVAE ELBO components, duplicate rate, marginal diversity, novel-goal generalization, and end-to-end success with a **frozen common scorer**. Do not choose the model by training reconstruction loss alone.

**Comparisons / controls:**

- goal-blind Q2-only vs task-conditioned repair-only;
- from scratch vs Q2 pretraining, matched architecture/compute;
- one successful assignment per root vs all known distinct assignments, with root balancing;
- optional observed-cost preference as an ablation, only if trustworthy witnessed costs exist;
- optionally unchanged-architecture goal-blind successful-repair cVAE to isolate whether the improvement came from **repair supervision or extra goal conditioning**.

### 5.5 Stage T3: collect/assemble candidate-matched Boltzmann training evidence

Fix a chosen candidate-generator candidate for this phase (or a small predeclared set for balanced training). On TRAIN roots, generate the **same type of directly constructed sequences** the network will face during deployment, then obtain X/Y from the unchanged independent teacher for a representative stratified sample.

- Retain archival successes, omissions, same-endpoint contrasting orders and V2 data, with source/weighting audit.
- Label a **diverse** subset: selected low-energy predicted repairs, near-threshold examples, high-scoring false positives, random other sequences, distinct endpoints, differing lengths/orders and candidate bands. Include some low-score examples to avoid score-selection bias. Fix the selection probabilities/seeds and store inclusion provenance.
- Deduplicate endpoint Y; query ordered X separately. For Y=0, some X can remain missing and be marginalized; however, retain a representative sample of Y=0 with observed X if its conditional factor needs supervision. Avoid making all Y=0 appear X=0 by default.
- Don't physically label every candidate and don't feed teacher results during learned inference. Use TRAIN root queries for weight fitting; holdout candidate labels for validation calibration/comparisons.
- A training-set positive from a new candidate source must be matched to **its actual complete ordered root context**, not labeled by a fixed-root hypothetical or by the old Q2 placement label.

### 5.6 Stage T4: retrain the SAME coupled F2/F3/F5 Boltzmann model

The primary X/Y objective is the **exact observed-data root negative log likelihood**—same as `xy.py`, including shared endpoint Y, repeated conditional X observations, and marginalization of missing X/Y. At one root:

\[
\mathcal L_{XY}(R)=-\log \sum_{\text{unobserved }Y}
\left[\prod_{s}P_\theta(Y_s)\right]
\left[\prod_{r\in\text{observed X}}P_\theta(X_r\mid Y_{s(r)})\right].
\]

Use only the necessary observed/masked factors; never count the same Y endpoint repeatedly as independent Y evidence because six X orders share it. Average at root level; compare source-stratified sampling/reweighting so a root with many queried orders does not overwhelm the rest. Preserve `F2` order, `F3` endpoint, `F5` coupling, fixed temperature/normalization and proven inference math.

Primary loss is **NLL alone**. **Optional ablation**, only if ranking remains poor despite better candidates: within-root pairwise ranking between verified `Z+=1` and `Z-=0` complete repairs, e.g.

\[
\mathcal L_{\rm rank}=\operatorname{softplus}(C_\theta(r^+)-C_\theta(r^-)+m),
\qquad \mathcal L=\mathcal L_{XY}+\lambda_{\rm rank}\mathcal L_{\rm rank}.
\]

`r+` and `r-` need actual X/Y outcomes and compatible root/cost definitions; do not derive labels from the model's own energy. Validate calibration before/after adding rank loss; prioritize accurate probability factorization. No extra third neural network is needed.

**Always preserve these arms as controls:** coupled D; uncoupled F5=0 E; order-invariant G; DIRECT joint-success predictor (where feasible to retrain on matching data). **Keep D as the proposed main model** even if an ablation is inconclusive; do not hide a null finding.

### 5.7 Stage T5: coordinated sequential refinement (if justified)

A **single main pass** is enough to establish the method: task-aware cVAE → direct candidate pool → labelled on-policy training distribution → retrained Boltzmann → end-to-end test.

If validation shows one of the components is still limiting, do a limited, recorded alternate update:

1. **Coverage failure:** revise task-conditioned cVAE training/sampling (including more distinct successes when teammate data arrives) while keeping the Boltzmann comparator frozen for diagnosis.
2. **Ranking failure:** hold sampler/constructor fixed; collect hard verified X/Y contrasts from *its* candidate distribution; retrain/calibrate D with archival data retained.
3. **Both poor:** alternate at most a preregistered small number of rounds, collecting fresh on-policy physical labels whenever the sampler/constructor distribution changes, and retaining old labels for stability.

No gradients through discrete `argmin`, no use of newly generated test outcomes as self-labels, no unverified Boltzmann energy as GT, and no guarantee that alternating updates converge to a global optimum.

### 5.8 Backward/outer optimization: what it means and when to use it

The **ideal training-only** question is: "Which compact action set would cause the *current* Boltzmann scorer to select a physically successful repair?" Formally:

\[
D^*_{\rm oracle}(R;\theta)=\arg\max_{D\subseteq P(R),\,|\mathcal R(D)|\le B_{score}}
Z^*\big(\arg\min_{r\in\mathcal R(D)} C_\theta(r)\big),
\]

where `P(R)` is a broad action pool and **physical X/Y labels** are required to evaluate the outer objective. This is **not** an executable test-time rule because its reward is privileged. On a partly labelled pool, treat the objective as *observed-pool oracle / unknown outside checked candidates*.

Use it, if helpful, only as **diagnostic/teacher signal** for a small parameterized analytical selection rule (`temperature`, mix weight, candidate allocations), tuned on TRAIN/validation outcomes. No new learned selector is required. If it yields no robust gain over the simple task-conditioned cVAE and fixed M, drop it. This formalizes the user's "work backward from what Boltzmann needs" idea without pretending to solve a combinatorial global optimum exactly.

Lack of discoveries under a finite new budget is **still not proof** of physical impossibility.

## 7. Evaluation: what proves that the method improved?

### 7.1 Core decomposition, with honest denominators

For each root and selected candidate set:

1. **Known-assignment ingredient recall:** whether all action pairs of ≥1 *previously verified* successful assignment appear in `D`. Useful as a cheap diagnostic; only measures **known** positives and ignores ordering/execution.
2. **Verified complete-repair candidate coverage / oracle@pool:** whether at least one **physically verified** X=1,Y=1 complete ordered sequence was found in the scored set. On a partially labelled pool, report **confirmed-success found** and **unresolved candidate fraction**, not a definitive absence claim.
3. **Conditional selection success:** among roots where ≥1 verified successful scored candidate is known, fraction for which the actual selected plan is physically verified successful. Attribute ranking failures only if the verified successful candidate was present and the selected plan had a decided failing result.
4. **Top-1 end-to-end task success:** X=1,Y=1 for **the ONE actually selected** original-root repair; this is the primary deployment metric on solvable K1–K3 roots. Count no-op and abstention separately.
5. **Repair success relative to available finalized K certificate:** report on the frozen `eval_repair` subset; do not call this proof of globally minimal K. Separately count verified shorter-than-legacy-K corrections.
6. **K0 no-op accuracy / unnecessary correction rate** and **NONE abstention correctness under the legacy protocol**; no universal infeasibility claim.
7. **Cost and time:** raw draws, distinct legal actions, candidate construction count, sequences scored per K, GPU scorer time, CPU verifier/teacher calls **separately**, total learned inference latency, peak VRAM, planner calls inside learned inference (=0), physically witnessed travel on successful cases, analytic displacement and chosen move count.
8. **Score quality:** root-consistent X/Y joint NLL, Brier, AUROC/AUPRC, ECE/reliability, matched-endpoint order ranking where physically labelled, high-confidence false positives. Use a **shared held-out scored/labelled candidate set** for architecture prediction metrics, not each model's favorite proposals.

Paired comparisons should use common root/seed assignments, root-/family-clustered bootstrap uncertainty and exact denominators. Report per-domain and per-class K1/K2/K3 breakdown, with K3 and hard-order cases called out separately. For time comparisons, include construction and scorer inference; label collection is **training cost** and must be disclosed separately.

### 7.2 Required experimental matrix (not all on the first day)

**A. Does direct construction work?** Old iterative V2, old bounded `oneshot` V2, and **new static direct construction** with the same frozen old sampler/scorer. Compare exact candidate counts and latency; don't use the old 2D 25.9% result as direct-construction proof.

**B. How much does more K buy us?** Fixed sampler/scorer; `M={4,8,12,16}` (optionally 2) with explicit `B_score` matched budgets and separately an uncapped small-n subset. Plot oracle coverage **and** top-1 success, since added candidates may introduce Boltzmann false positives.

**C. Does sampling rule help?** Current repeat-and-dedup vs true top-M MC marginal vs without-replacement vs temperature vs legal-uniform/band mixture, at matched raw draws, unique placements and scored-sequence caps. Isolate the rule from model retraining first.

**D. Does task-aware cVAE learning help?** Goal-blind Q2 only vs goal-conditioned repair-only from scratch vs Q2-initialized/combined, **same sampling rule** and `M/B_score`; compare one unique positive assignment/root vs all known distinct positives; include goal-ablated repair-only control if feasible.

**E. Is the Boltzmann distribution mismatch real and fixable?** Freeze each selected sampler and compare **V2 frozen D** versus **D retrained on GT-labelled candidates generated by that exact sampler**. Cross-evaluate old/new samplers against old/new scorers (at least a 2×2 comparison). Keep source-matched training budgets, compare probability calibration and true top-1 success.

**F. Does the explicit coupled factorization matter?** D vs E (`F5=0`), G (order-invariant) and DIRECT on a **shared candidate distribution/held-out candidate set**. Evaluate X/Y prediction and end-to-end outcome, not just NLL.

**G. Optional if justified:** root-balanced NLL + auxiliary verified within-root ranking loss; fixed vs entropy/difficulty-based **adaptive per-object M**; observed-cost-weighted cVAE; simple analytical selector tuned via verified outer objective. Each is a **one-axis ablation**, not piled onto the main method without isolation.

**H. Dataset expansion later:** base one-unique assignment vs base all-known assignments vs expanded 2–3 new verified assignments/root in selected cohort; matching training/selection conditions.

**I. Honest independent baselines:** analytical distance-band and uniform proposal controls; flow matching if resources permit; `gamma=0` no-travel comparator. If changing primary paper tables from archived plan, state explicitly this V3 method is a **revised study** and retain V1/V2 rows as historical baselines, not silently relabeled reruns.

### 7.3 Stopping rule and statistical choices

- Learn `no-op gate`, joint-success admission `eta`, `alpha, beta, gamma`, and any temperature/mixture coefficients on **training/validation only**. Default to simpler fixed rules if marginal gains are inconclusive; report when `gamma=0` wins.
- A classed test-root success requires **independent** physical verification X/Y. Store missingness; no optimistic imputation.
- Every model/sampler comparison must be at matched `B_score` or display a Pareto curve over budgets; don't claim an efficient sampler if it merely evaluated many more complete sequences.
- Report not just mean success but root-cluster confidence intervals and consistent seed schedules (single-seed smoke, ≥3 independent model/randomness seeds for main results where feasible).
- Do not pick models, select thresholds, add labels to training, or refine sampling based on already exposed held-out TEST outcomes. Acquire a fresh unseen final cohort for strong selection-free test claims if possible.

## 10. Scientific claim boundaries and definition of done

### 10.1 Claims allowed only after evidence

- **Can claim:** task-aware placements increase verified complete-repair candidate coverage or top-1 success **if demonstrated** under matched budgets, independent GT verification and proper uncertainty.
- **Can claim:** coupled F2/F3/F5 learns order-sensitive executable correction and endpoint goal restoration, **within the pinned physical-teacher contract**, if matched-endpoint order discrimination and held-out results support it.
- **Cannot claim:** automatically identified necessary blocker objects; identified causal DAG; universal feasibility proof; global minimum-energy physical plan; globally optimal successful assignment; guarantee that each cVAE proposal is robot-executable; success after an observed failure via online replanning; learned explicit intermediate joint states; superior travel when gamma was zero or witnesses missing; unbiased final performance on already reused TEST roots.
